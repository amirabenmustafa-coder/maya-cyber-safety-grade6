Maya and the Mystery Game Message

A short story about links, passwords, and asking for help.

Page 1 · The tempting message

A tempting message

After school, Maya opens her favourite game. A message pops up: “You’ve won 1,000 free coins! Claim them now!” Maya is excited, but she notices that she doesn’t recognize the sender.

What does the message promise?

Page 2 · Something feels wrong

Something feels wrong

The message says Maya must hurry and enter her username and password on a strange website. Her friend Leo says, “Let’s check with an adult first.” Maya remembers that real prizes shouldn’t require her password.

What is a warning sign?

Page 3 · Maya makes a safe choice

Maya makes a safe choice

Maya doesn’t click the link or reply. She closes the message and tells her mother what happened. Together, they check the game’s official website and find no such prize.

What should Maya do with the suspicious link?

Page 4 · Protecting her account

Protecting her account

Maya and her mother review her account settings. Maya learns to use a long, unique password and never share it, even with friends. With her mother’s help, she turns on an extra sign-in check.

Why is a unique password safer?

Page 5 · Maya helps Leo

Maya helps Leo

The next day, Leo receives a similar message. Maya tells him to pause, avoid the link, and show a trusted adult. They report the message through the game’s safety tools. Maya feels proud: one careful choice helped protect both friends.

What is the story’s main lesson?

Remember: Pause. Don’t click or share private information. Tell a trusted adult.
